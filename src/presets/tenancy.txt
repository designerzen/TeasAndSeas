ASSURED SHORTHOLD TENANCY AGREEMENT

This agreement is between the Landlord and the Tenant for the letting of Flat 3, 12 Harbour Row.

1. The Tenant shall pay the rent of nine hundred pounds on the first day of each month. If the rent is more than seven days late, the Tenant must pay a late fee and interest at eight percent.

2. The Landlord will protect the deposit in a government approved scheme within thirty days, and will return it within ten days of the end of the tenancy. The Landlord may deduct from the deposit the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant, but not fair wear and tear.

3. The Landlord shall keep the structure, the heating and the hot water in good repair, and will carry out urgent repairs promptly.

4. The Landlord may enter the property to inspect it, but only after giving the Tenant at least twenty four hours written notice, except in an emergency.

5. The Tenant must not sublet the property, keep pets or redecorate without the prior written consent of the Landlord. The Landlord will not refuse that consent unreasonably.

6. The Tenant is responsible for the cost of any professional cleaning that the Landlord considers necessary, in the Landlord's sole discretion.

7. After the first six months, either party may end the tenancy by giving two months written notice. The Tenant has the right to stay in the property until a court orders otherwise, and the Landlord shall not evict the Tenant without a court order.

8. The minimum term is six months. If the Tenant leaves before then, the Tenant must pay the rent for the rest of the minimum term and an early termination fee.

9. The Landlord shall not be liable for loss of or damage to the Tenant's belongings, and the Tenant is advised to insure them.
