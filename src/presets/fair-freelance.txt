FREELANCE DESIGN AGREEMENT

This is a plain agreement between you, the freelancer, and Kettle & Tide Studio (the "Client"). We have tried to make it fair to both sides.

1. The Client will pay you the agreed fee within fourteen days of each invoice. If a payment is late, the Client will pay you interest and will compensate you for reasonable costs of recovering it.

2. You retain ownership of all your sketches, working files and unused ideas. Once the final invoice is paid, the Client receives a licence to use the finished designs for the project described in the brief.

3. You have the right to show the finished work in your portfolio. The Client will credit you by name wherever that is practical.

4. Either of us may end this agreement with fourteen days written notice. If the Client cancels, the Client will pay you for all work completed up to that date, and any deposit paid for work not yet started is refundable.

5. The Client will not share your personal details with anyone without your consent, and will promptly notify you if anything goes wrong with them.

6. We will each keep the other's confidential information private. This protection is mutual and applies equally to both of us.

7. If the brief changes, we will agree any new fee together and in good faith before the extra work begins. You are entitled to say no.

8. Your total liability under this agreement is limited to the fee you have been paid. The Client is responsible for checking that the final designs are suitable before they are published.

9. If we disagree, we will first try to resolve it by talking. Nothing in this agreement takes away any right the law already gives you.
