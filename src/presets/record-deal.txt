EXCLUSIVE RECORDING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Hollow Sun Records Limited (the "Label") and the performer named in the schedule (the "Artist").

1. The Artist hereby assigns to the Label all rights, title and interest in every recording made during the Term, throughout the universe and in perpetuity, in any media now known or hereafter devised.

2. The Artist irrevocably waives all moral rights in the recordings and agrees that the Label may edit, remix or exploit them in its sole discretion without the consent of the Artist.

3. The Label will pay the Artist a royalty of eight percent of net receipts. The Label may deduct from that royalty all recording costs, video costs, tour support, marketing costs and any other sums which the Label considers appropriate, and no royalty is payable until every such cost has been recouped.

4. The Artist shall not record for any other person during the Term, and must not perform any of the compositions for five years after the Term ends.

5. The Term will last for one album and the Label may extend it, at any time and without notice, for up to six further albums. The Artist may not terminate this Agreement for any reason.

6. The Artist shall indemnify the Label against all claims, losses and expenses arising from the recordings, and the Label shall not be liable to the Artist for any failure to release or promote them.

7. The Label may assign this Agreement to anyone. The Artist may not.

8. Any dispute will be settled by binding arbitration in a place chosen by the Label, and the Artist waives the right to bring a claim in any court.

9. The Label will provide the Artist with a royalty statement twice a year, and the Artist has the right to access the accounts once a year on thirty days written notice, at the Artist's own expense.
